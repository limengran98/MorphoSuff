# Figure 1e methods

The three frozen exact same-cell crops, per-channel display windows, pixel size
and complete KO-response maps were retained. Each microscopy pair is rendered
with identical physical dimensions; scale bars are computed from the recorded
0.325-µm pixel size. Phase and target heatmaps share the same signed z-score
scale from −3 to 3. KO rows and feature-class strips use the frozen display
order. The three examples were predeclared elsewhere in the manuscript and are
not inferential replicates.
