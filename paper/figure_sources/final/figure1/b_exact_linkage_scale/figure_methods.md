# Figure 1b methods

All 73 OPS screens were enumerated from the frozen exact-linkage audit. For
each screen, the number of distinct phase cells with at least one exact-linked
reporter label was paired with the total number of exact-linked
phase-cell–reporter observations. Screens were ordered only by distinct linked
phase-cell count, with stable screen identifier breaking ties. The slate
phase-cell points and blue squares therefore share a screen-level statistical
unit. Blue squares and orange connectors are drawn only for screens containing
two to seven acquired reporters; in each single-reporter screen, the
reporter-observation count equals the phase-cell count exactly and is not
redundantly overplotted.

The second spectrum enumerates the 99 unique acquired
`reporter_slug::screen_id` assay keys. Each point is one reporter–screen assay
and is ordered by its raw gene–guide-concordant exact-linked reporter
observation count, with stable assay key breaking ties. LAMP1, FeRhoNox and pRb
are labelled using the same predeclared manuscript-example set used in panel
c. The assay values sum to 9,996,286 reporter observations. In a
multi-reporter screen, one phase cell can contribute to more than one assay;
the 9,996,286 reporter observations are therefore not 9,996,286 independent
cells.

The right frequency chart enumerates the five frozen reporter-block
dimensionalities (20, 24, 30, 60 and 72 endpoints) and their reporter counts.
Their count-weighted sum is 1,604 endpoints across 52 reporters. No prediction
result, recoverability rank, normalization, hypothesis test or uncertainty
interval entered either ordering. Endpoint dimensions are equally spaced
categories in ascending order; bar heights count reporters (1, 38, 6, 1, 6),
with a zero baseline and no error bars or fitted density. Blue fills match the
adjacent assay spectrum, with darker 0.70-pt outlines to retain visibility after
reduction; the categories are not joined by a trend line. Exact count annotations
sit above the bars with a fixed offset.

For integration beneath the full-width schematic, the two spectra and endpoint
frequency chart share a 183 × 30 mm row with separate axis gutters. The
standalone plot contains axis labels and keys, without an explanatory heading
or panel letter.
Native PDF/SVG text is editable; body and tick labels are at least 6 pt and
axis labels 6.5 pt. The screen key occupies a dedicated band
outside the data axes, so reporter-total marks cannot intersect its labels.
Before export, every visible text artist is checked against all other text
artists and the physical figure canvas. The final PDF compositor assigns the
9-pt b in a reserved top-left area and rejects letter/text collisions after
all panel transformations; the manuscript proof receives the same check.
