# Figure 1b legend

**b, Exact-linkage depth across physical screens and acquired reporter assays.**
The left ranked spectrum enumerates 73 physical screens. The slate curve and
points show unique exact-linked phase cells for every screen; blue squares and
orange connectors show reporter-observation totals only for the 17
multi-reporter screens. In each of the other 56 screens, the two counts are
identical, so a redundant overplotted square is omitted. The middle spectrum resolves the reporter-observation
total into 99 acquired reporter–screen assays; labelled points locate the
predeclared LAMP1, FeRhoNox and pRb examples. The right frequency chart shows
1, 38, 6, 1 and 6 reporters with 20, 24, 30, 60 and 72 endpoints, respectively.
These 52 reporter blocks comprise 1,604 endpoints; bar labels are exact reporter
counts, not endpoint totals. All three plots are descriptive enumerations.
