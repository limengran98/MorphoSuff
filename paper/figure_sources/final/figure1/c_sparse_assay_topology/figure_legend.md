### Figure 1c | Ninety-nine assays form a sparse reporter-screen design

The matrix enumerates all 99 observed reporter-screen assays linking 52 targeted reporters to 73 physical OPS screens. Each glyph is one unique reporter-screen assay; glyph area is proportional to the number of gene-guide-concordant exact-linked reporter observations, and colour denotes the targeted biological system. The aligned top track reports reporters per screen; each unit-height coloured segment is one reporter. Screens are separated into 56 single-reporter and 17 multi-reporter experiments, while the right-hand lollipop track reports the number of screens available for each reporter using the same system colours. Black rings identify the predeclared LAMP1, FeRhoNox and pRb examples in the acquisition matrix. Registered square phase and reporter crops show a BORCS7-knockout LAMP1 assay and an ALG12-knockout FeRhoNox assay; leaders connect each crop to the corresponding assay glyph and scale bars denote 20 micrometres. OPS0043 and OPS0077 are the two highest-occupancy screens, containing seven and six co-acquired reporters, respectively; their labels point to the corresponding occupancy stacks. Blank matrix positions denote reporter-screen combinations that were not assayed, not negative measurements.
