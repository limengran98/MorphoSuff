# Figure 1d legend

**d, Reporter-specific KO-response atlas.** Rows are the 52 targeted reporters
in the frozen manuscript order and columns are the 1,000 gene knockouts in the
frozen response order. Colour denotes the within-reporter response percentile;
blue, white and red indicate low, median and high relative response,
respectively. The upper trace is the mean response percentile across reporters.
The predeclared FeRhoNox, LAMP1 and pRb cases are labelled.
