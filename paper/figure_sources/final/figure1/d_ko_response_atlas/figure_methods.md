# Figure 1d methods

The frozen 52-by-1,000 KO-response percentile matrix and reporter ordering were
used without re-estimation. Values remain within-reporter percentiles on the
interval 0–1. A blue–white–red colormap is centred at percentile 0.5 to share a
visual language with panel e; this colour choice does not convert the values to
signed response z-scores. The upper trace is the column-wise mean percentile.
No clustering, filtering, uncertainty interval or hypothesis test was added.
