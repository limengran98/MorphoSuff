# Figure 2 legend

**a,** Same-cell phenotype prediction and schematic field, gene and whole-screen
holdouts, with reporter identity retained across screens. Field images are
illustrative cell-centred crops, not complete fields or actual partition
assignments; scale bars, 20 µm.
**b,** Reporter-level distributions across ten methods, with KO-response Pearson
correlation on the left and cell-level Pearson correlation on the right throughout b–d. Boxes show the median
and interquartile range; whiskers span the 5th–95th percentiles and points are reporter
values. **c,** Physical screens ranked by ten-method consensus for both evaluation
resolutions; shaded envelopes retain the frozen
between-method spread, and the adjacent summaries compare interquartile spread across
screens and across methods. **d,** Reporter-by-screen assay views for both evaluation
resolutions. Grey connectors join assays from the same
reporter. Field, gene and whole-screen denote the same frozen generalization tests in
all panels.

Reporter-level comparisons pair the same reporters against the prespecified MLP
(n = 52 for field/gene and 34 for whole-screen transfer), using two-sided Wilcoxon
signed-rank tests, Holm correction across 54 comparisons and an absolute median
paired-effect threshold of 0.02. Reporter scores are arithmetic means over five
folds or eligible destination directions. The corrected primary inference table accompanies
the figure; no significance stars are drawn. Screen and assay summaries are
descriptive. For field/gene cell-level screen and assay views, frozen reporter-level
scores are reweighted by physical membership; the 99 assay marks resolve 52 distinct
reporter-level values. Whole-screen cell scores are evaluated directly in destination
cells. Between-screen spread combines target composition and acquisition context.
