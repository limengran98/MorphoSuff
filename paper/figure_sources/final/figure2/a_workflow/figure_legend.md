# Figure 2a legend

**a, Same-cell phenotype prediction and generalization tests.** Ten methods
map 172 phase-morphology features to 20–72 reporter-specific phenotype
endpoints. The real pair is pRb–FECH from OPS0077; scale bars, 20 µm.
Evaluation withholds entire imaging fields, all cells of test knockout genes,
or complete destination screens with source screens carrying the same
reporter. Train/test colours illustrate split units rather than actual fold
assignments; validation is omitted. Field images are cell-centred views,
explicitly labelled "Example cell crops", not complete fields. KO A/B/C are
generic gene groups, and cell/plate cartoons are schematic.
