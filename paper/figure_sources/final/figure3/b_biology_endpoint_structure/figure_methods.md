Biological-system summaries use reporters as the statistical unit and the frozen ten-model-median held-out-gene score. Endpoint-family summaries use reporter–endpoint-family rows from the frozen endpoint audit. Biological-system and endpoint-family intervals are the previously frozen deterministic bootstrap summaries; the latter do not represent a ten-model endpoint-level consensus.

## Analysis specification

- Biological-system statistical unit: reporter (n=52 overall; 1–9 per system).
- Endpoint-family statistical unit: reporter–endpoint-family row (118 rows across 52 reporters and six families).
- Biological-system intervals are the frozen reporter-level bootstrap summaries.
- Endpoint-family intervals are the frozen deterministic 10,000-draw audit summaries.
- The endpoint-family archive is not represented as a ten-model endpoint-level consensus.
- No group is hidden because of its score or sample size; n is shown for every row.
