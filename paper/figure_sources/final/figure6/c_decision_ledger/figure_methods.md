# Figure 6c methods

The statistical unit is the reporter (n=52); 43 pass the independent reliability floor. Recovery and response fidelity are evaluated on one coherent checkpoint-average median ensemble. The upper matrix shows unchanged counterfactual-response utility metrics. The lower matrix shows signed scaled distances to five frozen criteria: (value−threshold)/(1−threshold) for the four lower-bound gates, and min(variance−0.5,1.5−variance)/0.5 for the amplitude band. Negative margins fail the corresponding quantitative criterion; ranking decisions have their separately declared recall threshold.

The local portable statistics builder assigns tiers in order: reliability failure → not identifiable; missing required fidelity → unresolved; quantitative; ranking; recovery below 0.60 → measurement required; otherwise unresolved. Ranking precedes measurement-required when both rules apply. All 52 baseline tiers remain 30 quantitative, 7 ranking, 3 measurement-required, 9 not-identifiable and 3 unresolved.

Eight cutoffs are each perturbed singly by ±0.05 absolute units and ±10% relative units (32 scenarios), then all eight together (four scenarios). Variance upper-bound direction is reversed; the declared measurement-required upper cutoff follows the original perturbation convention. The inset is the row-normalized 5×5 transition table across 36 scenarios; exact assignments, counts and summaries are released alongside it.
