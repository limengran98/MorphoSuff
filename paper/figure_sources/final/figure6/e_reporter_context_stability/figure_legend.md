**b, Within-reporter stability across screen contexts.** Pale circles show the unweighted ten-method benchmark score median for each directed destination-screen evaluation (81 directions across 34 repeated reporters), not a score of the response-replacement ensemble; diamonds show medians across destinations and horizontal segments show observed ranges.
