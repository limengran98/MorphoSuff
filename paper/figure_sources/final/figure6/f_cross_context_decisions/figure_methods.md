# Figure 6f methods

The panel deterministically renders deposited, panel-local gate summaries. The OPS
atlas row uses the unweighted median across the 52 final reporter rows for each gate;
the thin pale ticks retain the individual reporter values. OASIS and PERISCOPE rows
retain their reported context, unit and frozen threshold values. No cross-context
model is fitted, no gate is re-estimated and no pooled effect size is calculated by
the builder. The panel's statistical role is descriptive comparison of measurement
contexts, not meta-analysis.
