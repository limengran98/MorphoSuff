# Supplementary Figure 4a legend

Strict nested leave-one-reporter-out calibration of scientific utility. Each point is one of 52 reporters and is coloured by the frozen measurement-sufficiency tier. The main plot compares observed utility with the prediction obtained when that reporter is excluded from model fitting; the dashed line denotes identity and marginal densities summarize observed and predicted distributions. Labels mark the four largest absolute outer-LOO residuals according to a pre-specified deterministic rule. The lower plot shows prediction-minus-observation residuals against mean utility; the solid teal line denotes mean bias and dashed lines denote the empirical mean plus or minus 1.96 standard deviations. The forest plot shows reporter-level Spearman associations of retained utility with recoverability, response-rank fidelity, amplitude fidelity and within-screen reliability; intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 10,000 reporter-level resamples.

Supplementary Figure 4a and Figure 6b use utility targets referenced to the other 51 reporters within each outer holdout, rather than the all-reporter percentile index in Figure 6a,c. For rank statistics only, utility is rounded to 12 decimal places before average ranks are assigned to ties; the same rule applies to all reporter-bootstrap resamples.
