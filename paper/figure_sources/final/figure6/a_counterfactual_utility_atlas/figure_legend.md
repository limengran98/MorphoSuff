# Figure 6a legend

Counterfactual response replacement reveals reporter-specific scientific utility. Rows denote the 52 frozen reporters and columns denote response-rank correlation, recall of the strongest 5% of responses, and overlap of the top Gene Ontology biological-process, cellular-component and protein-complex results after replacing the observed held-out response with its phase-derived proxy. Marginal half-violins show the reporter-level distributions on the common 0–1 scale; black bars and vertical segments denote the median and interquartile range. The ordered heatmap retains each reporter as the statistical unit. The right rail shows the frozen scientific utility index, coloured by measurement-sufficiency tier; selected reporters are labelled by a deterministic manuscript list. Left strips denote biological system and final tier.
