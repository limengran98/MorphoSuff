# Figure 4c legend

Observed and predicted heatmaps show the same 12 frozen held-out gene×screen
profiles. Predicted values are the element-wise unweighted median of ten frozen
models. Scatterplots retain all 1,000 held-out genes per reporter. The dashed
diagonal denotes equality. The pRb SALL3 microscopy inset is the registered
exact same cell linked to the highlighted magnitude point; the scale bar is
20 µm. Heatmap limits are reporter-specific and printed on each color bar.
