# Figure 4 | Same-cell morphology carries reporter-specific signal, but quantitative fidelity and transfer remain separable

**a,** Full-atlas information-intervention analysis using the frozen reporter-specific MLP. Each point is one reporter (*n* = 52) and is the arithmetic mean across five frozen held-out-gene folds. Split rainclouds show cell-level (circles) and KO-level (squares) Pearson correlation for exact same-cell pairing, within-gene×screen shuffling, covariate-matched shuffling, size/shape-only input and the 172-dimensional representation after removal of size/shape features. The adjacent effect clouds show reporter-level exact-minus-control differences; large open symbols and horizontal intervals denote the frozen mean effect and reporter-resampled 95% bootstrap interval. **b,** Exact-pair cell-level recovery versus recovery of cross-fitted within-gene×screen residuals. Lines join the same reporter, ordered by residual recovery; the marginal clouds show the paired distributions and the right column shows reporter-wise differences. **c,** Three preselected response-reconstruction modes. Heatmaps show the 12 frozen highest-response held-out gene×screen profiles for observed phenotypes and the element-wise unweighted median prediction across the frozen ten-method roster. Full-range magnitude plots include all 1,000 held-out genes per reporter; the pRb zoom additionally shows 0–5 on both axes, without changing the statistics. LysoTracker retains quantitative magnitude, ChromaLIVE 488 retains much of the response pattern, whereas pRb preserves rank information while strongly compressing amplitude. The pRb SALL3 phase–reporter crop is the registered exact same cell linked to the highlighted magnitude point. Color ranges are reporter-specific and stated beneath each heatmap pair. **d,** Physical whole-screen transfer. Curves show two preselected reporter-to-destination-screen directions and three representative predictors. The aligned raincloud summaries retain all 81 transfer directions for each of the ten frozen methods and separately display KO-response reconstruction and strong-response magnitude ranking. Method identities are estimators, not biological replicates.
