Magnitude-ranking fidelity and quantitative amplitude fidelity are shown for the same 52 reporters in a shared order. Reporters are ordered by the predicted-to-observed KO-response variance ratio. The upper track shows KO magnitude-rank Spearman correlation and the lower track shows the variance ratio; stems in the lower track originate at the prespecified 0.5 boundary. Crimson denotes the 11 reporters retaining less than half of observed response variance and teal denotes the remaining reporters. Ten of the 11 compressed reporters retain magnitude-rank Spearman correlation above 0.5. The lower colour strip identifies the reporter biological system. Labels are restricted to the predeclared pRb, p53, TOMM20, NPM1 and LysoTracker examples.
