For each fixed low-stability reporter, agreement was evaluated across the three available well pairs, three deterministic well-balanced independent-guide-half partitions and 100 well-balanced random-cell-half resampling draws. Individual values are shown. Small-n well and guide comparisons are summarized by the median and observed range; random-cell-half draws are summarized by the median and interquartile range and are displayed as resampling texture rather than biological replication. The pRb images are frozen, outcome-agnostic phase/geometry medoids from wells A1, A2 and A3 for the same SALL3 guide. Phase and pRb channels share target-specific pooled display limits across the three cells. Images are registered within each well; scale bars are 20 µm. The microscopy is descriptive and was not used to select or quantify the repeatability cases.

## Analysis specification

- Statistical units: three well pairs; three deterministic independent-guide-half partitions; 100 random-cell-half resampling draws per reporter.
- Fixed cases: pRb and NPM1 were prespecified by the upstream low-stability rule; they were not selected from the appearance of this panel.
- Missingness and exclusions: none in the frozen plotted tables.
- Summaries: median/range for the three well or guide values; median/IQR for the 100 resampling draws.
- Resampling boundary: random-cell-half draws are not independent biological replicates and are not used for a hypothesis-test star.
- Microscopy provenance: frozen selection manifest; pRb SALL3 guide across wells A1, A2 and A3; outcome-agnostic soft-QC non-edge phase/geometry medoids.
- Asset integrity: the exact three plotted NPZ assets are listed with SHA-256 hashes in `figure_source_microscopy_asset_manifest.csv`.
- Microscopy display: target-pooled 1st–99th percentile windows; 0.325 µm per pixel; 20 µm scale bars.
- Can say: low recoverability can coincide with poor agreement across wells and guide partitions even when random cell halves are more consistent.
- Cannot say: the images diagnose the cause of the instability or establish prevalence across reporters.
