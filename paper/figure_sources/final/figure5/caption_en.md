# Figure 5 | Distinct constraints limit morphology-based measurement sufficiency.

**a,** Reporter-level associations between held-out-gene recoverability and within-screen reliability, guide-half consistency, cross-screen consistency and exact-link coverage. Points denote Spearman correlations and lines 95% reporter-bootstrap confidence intervals from 3,000 resamples. The lower triangle gives pairwise-complete correlations among the four constraints and the upper triangle the corresponding reporter counts. The observed in-sample variance explained by the prespecified three-term model (within-screen reliability, guide-half consistency and log10 exact-link coverage; *n*=35) is compared with a 3,000-permutation null; cross-screen consistency is not a model term. **b,** KO-response agreement for pRb and NPM1 across three well pairs, three deterministic independent-guide-half partitions and 100 random-cell-half draws. Small-*n* comparisons show all three values, their median and range; random cell halves show 100 resampling draws with the median and interquartile range. Registered images show outcome-agnostic medoid cells for the same SALL3 guide in pRb wells A1--A3. Images provide screen context and are not the correlation units. **c,** Magnitude-rank fidelity and predicted-to-observed KO-response variance across all 52 reporters, ordered by variance ratio. Eleven reporters retain less than half of the observed response variance; ten of these eleven retain a magnitude-rank Spearman correlation above 0.5. The lower strip denotes biological system. **d,** Whole-screen transfer loss across 81 directed reporter-to-destination-screen evaluations nested in 34 reporters, ordered from lowest to highest loss and aligned to phase and targeted-phenotype non-targeting-control (NTC) shifts. The lower strip denotes biological system. The forest plot shows direction-level Spearman correlations with reporter-cluster bootstrap 95% confidence intervals; P values use 3,000 permutations of reporter-median shift summaries, with plus-one correction. Diamonds identify the two frozen FeRhoNox destination directions; registered NTC medoid images were selected using a fixed phase/geometry rule without reference to transfer performance. The association and images do not establish a causal mechanism or its prevalence. **e,** Conditional target-state ambiguity for the engineered 172-dimensional morphology representation and a raw-thumbnail sensitivity representation. Each point is one gene-level nearest-neighbour endpoint-discrepancy ratio relative to random matching (5,990 ratios in total); the raw-thumbnail neighbours are selected in a 32-dimensional principal-component representation of the thumbnails. Envelopes show distributions, open circles medians, thick lines interquartile ranges and thin lines 10th--90th percentiles. The dashed line at one marks the random-match reference. The raw-thumbnail analysis is a sensitivity representation, not an upper bound on raw-image learning. Scale bars, 20 μm.
