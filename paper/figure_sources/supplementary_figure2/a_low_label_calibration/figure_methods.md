The low-label source table contains ten methods evaluated at three target-label fractions on the same frozen 12-reporter panel and the same five held-out-gene folds. The 100% points come from a matched, independently frozen full-label evaluation of those 12 reporters. For each method and fraction, the panel displays the arithmetic mean and standard deviation of the five fold-level gene-macro endpoint Pearson correlations deposited in this Supplementary Figure 2 source bundle. Relative recovery is the displayed mean divided by that method's independent 100% mean. Relative log-AULC is the trapezoidal mean of relative recovery over log10 label fraction from 0.1% to 20%. It is a descriptive calibration summary, not a hypothesis test or an ordering of biological replicates. No method is selected or excluded on the basis of the displayed values.
