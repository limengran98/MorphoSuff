# Supplementary Figure 1 | Complete three-context evidence audit underlying the measurement decisions

**a,** All 52 OPS reporters evaluated against the five frozen criteria for the coherent checkpoint-average median ensemble. Recoverability is the arithmetic fivefold mean of endpoint-macro Pearson for that ensemble. Columns are reporters ordered by final decision tier; the upper strip denotes quantitative proxy, ranking proxy, measurement required, not identifiable and unresolved assignments. Colour gives signed scaled margins, blue above and red below the threshold. Exact values, identities and order accompany the source data. **b,** Complete evidence for the two capability-matched public applications, their input-permutation controls and four-dye substitution sensitivity. Small ticks are registered fold, screen or dye estimates; filled points are registered summaries and vertical lines are frozen thresholds. Dye substitutions are sensitivity analyses, not admitted targeted-readout applications. Admission and batch decisions are reported in Supplementary Tables 1 and 3.
