# Supplementary Figure 3 legend

**Predictor choice changes measurement-sufficiency assignments.** **a,** Complete
reporter-by-predictor tiers using the same held-out-gene evaluation, common control
scale and frozen decision rules. The 52 reporters retain the Figure 6 order and
are displayed down two consecutive 26-row blocks. Ten individual methods use the
checkpoint-average evaluation state where available; the separated ensemble column
is the coherent element-wise median predictor used as the manuscript reference.
Recovery and fidelity are calculated from each predictor's own aligned response
profiles. Letters and colours denote quantitative proxy (Q), ranking proxy (R),
measurement required (M), not identifiable (N) and unresolved (U). Daggers identify
the nine reporters whose observed-target reliability is below 0.30; these fixed
N assignments are excluded from the method-stability denominator. Among the
remaining 43 reporters, 20 support a proxy under every method, 6 under no method
and 17 cross the proxy/non-proxy boundary. **b,** LAMP1 response evidence under MLP,
MIDAS and scButterfly, selected after the analysis to illustrate Q, R and M
assignments. Points show reporter-level metrics; pale bands and solid boundaries
mark quantitative criteria. Dotted boundaries mark recovery 0.60 for the
measurement-required rule and hit recall 0.50 for the ranking rule. LAMP1
reliability is 0.448 for all three methods. Ranking can remain supported when
quantitative criteria fail; unresolved is not equivalent to measurement required.
The complete continuous evidence, both checkpoint states, reference-relative
changes and definition sensitivity are provided in Supplementary Data 1.

No inferential intervals or tests are shown: methods and states are descriptive
sensitivity axes, not biological replicates.
